8. PARTICIPANT RIGHTS

The participant understands that:

- This authorization is voluntary.
- The participant may refuse to sign this form.
- The participant may revoke this authorization in writing at any time.
- Revocation does not affect information already shared before the written revocation was received.
- Signing this form does not create a requirement to accept services.
- Signing this form does not create a supervisory, medical, clinical, or custodial relationship.
- The program operator provides housing/program coordination only unless otherwise stated in a separate written agreement.

9. LIMITATION OF AUTHORIZATION

This form does not authorize the program operator to act as the participant’s guardian, representative payee, medical decision-maker, legal representative, or supervising authority.

This form only permits communication for the limited purpose described in this authorization.
