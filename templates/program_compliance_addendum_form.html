1. APPLICABILITY

This Addendum applies when a participant is placed within the Program through:

  • U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs programs, including Grant and Per Diem (GPD) or VA-aligned referrals
  • Continuum of Care (CoC) Program placements
  • Correctional reentry, probation, parole, DOC, or justice-involved referral pathways
  • Any publicly funded, referral-based, or program-supported housing pathway requiring enhanced compliance standards

This Addendum supplements the applicable Master License Agreement and governs only in such cases.

This Addendum is intended solely to document program-level compliance alignment and coordination requirements. It does not create, expand, or modify housing rights, ownership rights, tenancy rights, or property-control rights.

2. DUAL-ENTITY PROGRAM STRUCTURE

The Program may operate through a dual-entity structure consisting of:

  • A property owner, housing provider, or property-control entity responsible for property-related operations, occupancy authorization, or property obligations assigned by written agreement
  • A nonprofit or program operator entity responsible for program participation, coordination, referral communication, compliance tracking, and documentation requirements

The participant understands these functions may be managed separately while operating together within a unified transitional housing framework.

3. NATURE OF OCCUPANCY AND PROGRAM ALIGNMENT

The Program maintains a structured transitional housing model designed to coordinate with referral systems, funding requirements, documentation standards, and program-alignment expectations.

The Program may remain compatible with multiple referral systems simultaneously while maintaining a uniform housing structure.

Participation in this Program may be connected to referral agencies, funding sources, case managers, VA representatives, CoC partners, DOC or reentry contacts, probation or parole contacts, or other approved coordinating agencies.

The Program Operator may maintain records, signed forms, participation notes, incident documentation, referral documentation, discharge documentation, and coordination records when necessary for program alignment and lawful operation.

4. PARTICIPANT RIGHTS AND PROTECTIONS

The participant has the right to fair and non-discriminatory treatment, written notice of program expectations, reasonable notice prior to standard program termination when applicable, access to a grievance or complaint process, privacy and dignity consistent with program rules and applicable law, and freedom from retaliation for submitting a grievance or complaint.

5. NOTICE AND TERMINATION PROCEDURES

Standard program termination may require reasonable notice, when applicable, depending upon applicable law, funding requirements, referral requirements, or written program policy.

Immediate program action, urgent discharge review, or safety intervention may occur consistent with applicable law and written program procedure when there is serious safety risk, violence, illegal activity, weapons possession, severe property damage, threats to others, or conduct creating urgent risk to participants, staff, or property.

Program discharge or removal does not independently determine housing status. Housing status is governed separately by the applicable Master License Agreement or other housing agreement.

6. GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE

Participants may submit written grievances regarding program conditions, rule enforcement, program decisions, discharge concerns, or staff conduct.

The Program Operator will review grievances within a reasonable timeframe and may document the concern, response, and any corrective action taken.

If the participant was referred by an outside agency, the participant may also contact the referral source or coordinating agency when appropriate.

7. PROGRAM PARTICIPATION REQUIREMENTS

The participant agrees to engage in written program expectations, participate in coordination when applicable, comply with house standards and safety expectations, cooperate with reasonable documentation requests, attend scheduled meetings/check-ins/reviews when required, and maintain respectful conduct toward others and the property.

The participant understands that referral-based or publicly funded placement may require documentation requests, scheduled check-ins, eligibility reviews, transition planning, case coordination, and program-status updates.

The participant remains responsible for complying with requirements imposed by external agencies, courts, probation or parole conditions, VA programs, funding sources, or referral partners.

The Program Operator may document participation, communicate with authorized sources, and maintain records necessary to preserve program alignment.

8. VA / DOC / REENTRY / COC PROGRAM COORDINATION

The Program Operator may coordinate with case managers, VA representatives, CoC partners, probation or parole contacts, DOC or reentry contacts, funding administrators, and service providers.

This coordination occurs only when authorized by release of information, referral agreement, funding requirement, or applicable law.

The Program Operator does not act as law enforcement, probation or parole officer, clinical or treatment provider, VA decision-maker, or benefits approval authority.

The Program Operator may provide housing-related and program-participation documentation when properly authorized.

9. FINANCIAL STRUCTURE CLARIFICATION

Program funding, referral support, grants, per diem payments, participant contributions, or other approved funding sources may support housing-related program costs when applicable.

Nothing in this Addendum guarantees funding approval, VA approval, grant approval, public assistance, rental assistance, benefits approval, permanent housing, or continued program placement.

The Program Operator may assist with coordination and documentation, but final determinations are made by outside entities.

10. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW

This Addendum shall be interpreted in accordance with applicable federal, state, and local law.

If any outside requirement conflicts with applicable law or protected rights, applicable law shall control.

11. RELATIONSHIP TO MASTER LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Addendum supplements the applicable Master License Agreement for participants placed through referral-based or publicly funded pathways.

Program participation and housing occupancy are related but distinct matters.

This Addendum governs program compliance alignment only.

Housing status is governed separately by the applicable housing agreement.

Nothing in this Addendum creates a landlord-tenant relationship, independent housing rights, a clinical or custodial relationship, or a guarantee of continued placement.

12. PARTICIPANT ACKNOWLEDGMENT
