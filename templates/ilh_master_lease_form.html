MASTER LEASE AGREEMENT

Independent Shared Housing – Property Control Structure

NILPF Framework

Document Version: v2.0 (2026)

This Master Lease Agreement is entered into by and between the Property Owner and the Program Operator for the lease of residential real property to be used solely as independent, non-supervised shared residential occupancy.

The Property shall not be used for medical care, assisted living, residential treatment, supervision, monitoring, or regulated services.

This Agreement establishes a property lease only and does not create any care, custodial, supervisory, service, or program relationship.
