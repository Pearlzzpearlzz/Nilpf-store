This agreement outlines the participant’s role within a structured housing program environment.

Participation in this program does not create a tenancy relationship and does not establish entitlement to housing beyond program guidelines.

The participant agrees to follow program structure, maintain respectful conduct, and uphold the expectations outlined by the program operator.

The operator maintains administrative oversight of the program but does not provide clinical care, supervision, or personal management services.

Participation is voluntary and may be discontinued in accordance with program guidelines.

By signing below, the participant acknowledges understanding and agreement to program participation expectations.
