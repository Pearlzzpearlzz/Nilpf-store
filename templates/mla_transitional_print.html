2. PURPOSE

This Agreement establishes a structured transitional housing arrangement designed to support individuals reentering the community, maintaining housing stability, and progressing toward independent living.

The Program provides shared housing within a structured environment focused on accountability, stability, personal responsibility, and successful transition.

This Agreement governs the participant’s housing license and occupancy status. Program compliance, agency coordination, funding responsibility, referral requirements, and service planning may be documented separately.

3. NATURE OF OCCUPANCY

Participant is granted a revocable, non-exclusive license to occupy an assigned sleeping space within the residence.

This Agreement does not create a landlord-tenant relationship unless required by applicable law, does not grant exclusive possession of any bedroom or unit, does not create ownership rights or permanent housing rights, and does not guarantee continued occupancy outside the terms of this Agreement and related program documents.

Sleeping arrangements may be shared, may be reassigned at the discretion of the Operator, and are subject to program structure, safety needs, occupancy needs, and administrative review.

Common areas are shared.

4. PROGRAM PARTICIPATION

Participant agrees to engage in reasonable program expectations, which may include maintaining communication with program staff or designated contacts, participating in goal-setting and progress discussions, complying with documentation and check-in requirements, following house standards and shared living expectations, and engaging in activities that support stability, accountability, and independence.

Failure to maintain reasonable participation may result in review of continued occupancy.

Program participation does not constitute supervision, medical care, clinical care, personal care, case management by the Operator, or custodial oversight.

5. INDEPENDENT LIVING STATUS

The Program provides housing only.

Unless separately stated in a written agreement, the Operator does not provide medical care, clinical services, personal care, supervision, monitoring, custodial care, financial control, or payee services.

Participant remains fully responsible for personal decisions, healthcare, finances, medication management, daily living activities, personal conduct, and outside appointments and obligations.

Participants may independently arrange outside services. The Operator is not responsible for services provided by outside agencies, providers, case managers, sponsors, or referral partners.

The residence is not a healthcare facility.

6. ADMINISTRATIVE AUTHORITY

The Operator retains administrative control of the residence for purposes of safety, maintenance, program structure, shared housing operations, documentation, occupancy coordination, and enforcement of house standards.

This may include reassignment of sleeping areas, enforcement of house rules, review of program participation, documentation of incidents or concerns, and coordination with authorized referral, funding, or support contacts when applicable.

Administrative authority does not create a supervisory, clinical, custodial, or caregiving relationship.

7. HOUSE STANDARDS

Participant agrees to comply with reasonable standards related to cleanliness, safety, respect for shared space, respectful conduct, quiet enjoyment of shared living areas, guest rules, property rules, fire safety, and privacy of other participants/members.

Violations may result in corrective action, program review, reassignment, discharge review, or termination of the license.

8. FINANCIAL CONTRIBUTION / FUNDING RESPONSIBILITY

Program costs, occupancy-related contributions, placement support, or other approved charges may be paid by the participant, a sponsoring agency, a referral source, a grant-funded program, a nonprofit sponsor, a public funding source, a VA-related source, a DOC/reentry-related source, or another approved responsible party.

The participant is responsible only for the financial contribution or payment obligation that has been separately disclosed in writing and agreed to as part of the participant’s placement, funding arrangement, sponsorship record, payor record, billing record, or other written program documentation.

9. TERM AND REVIEW

Participation is transitional and subject to periodic review.

Length of stay may be based on program progress, housing stability, funding or sponsorship status, referral or agency requirements, participant conduct, house capacity, safety considerations, and availability of appropriate placement.

The Operator may review continued occupancy periodically or when a significant program, safety, funding, documentation, or conduct issue arises.

10. TERMINATION / REVOCATION OF LICENSE

This license may be revoked or terminated for reasons including, but not limited to noncompliance with program expectations, failure to reasonably participate, violation of house standards, safety concerns, behavior disrupting the shared environment, threats, violence, harassment, serious misconduct, failure to comply with required documentation, non-payment only where a documented payment obligation applies, loss or change of required funding/referral support/sponsorship/program eligibility when applicable, or other written program reasons consistent with applicable law.

Participant agrees to vacate as required by applicable law and program procedure.

Nothing in this Agreement waives any rights, notices, or procedures required by applicable federal, state, or local law.

11. RISK AND RESPONSIBILITY

Participant assumes responsibility for personal property, personal conduct, personal decisions, outside relationships, outside services, daily living choices, and compliance with personal legal, court, agency, medical, or benefit obligations.

The Operator is not responsible for loss or damage of personal belongings, actions of other participants/members, outcomes related to personal decisions, services provided by outside agencies or providers, or participant failure to attend outside appointments or comply with outside requirements.

12. FAIR HOUSING AND NONDISCRIMINATION

The Program operates in compliance with applicable fair housing, disability, civil rights, and nondiscrimination laws, including where applicable the Fair Housing Act, Americans with Disabilities Act, Section 504 if applicable, and applicable state and local laws.

Reasonable accommodation requests may be reviewed according to applicable law and program procedure.

13. NO PARTNERSHIP / NO EMPLOYMENT RELATIONSHIP

This Agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture, employment relationship, landlord-tenant relationship unless required by applicable law, clinical provider relationship, custodial relationship, representative payee relationship, or case management relationship by the Operator unless separately agreed in writing.

14. RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER DOCUMENTS

This Agreement may be used together with other program documents, including but not limited to House Rules, Program Compliance Addendum, Program Participation Agreement, Payor / Funding Responsibility Record, Referral Source Record, Agency Sponsorship Record, Release of Information if applicable, Emergency Contact forms, and safety and shared housing documents.

If another document addresses program participation, referral obligations, funding, sponsorship, or outside agency coordination, that document supplements this Agreement but does not replace the housing license terms stated here unless expressly stated in writing.
