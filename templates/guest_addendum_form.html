09 – Guest Addendum

Shared Housing Program

© 2026 Pearlzz LLC. All Rights Reserved.

PURPOSE

This Guest Addendum outlines expectations and responsibilities related to guests within shared housing program.

This document applies to shared housing only and does not create supervision, services, or program oversight.

GUEST DEFINITION

A guest is any individual invited onto the premises by a participant who is not an approved participant of the housing.

PARTICIPANT RESPONSIBILITY FOR GUESTS

Participants are solely responsible for their guests, including:

Guest behavior and conduct
Compliance with house rules and community standards
Any damage caused by the guest
Ensuring guests do not interfere with the rights of others

GUEST LIMITATIONS

Guests may not:

Live in the residence without written approval
Receive mail at the property
Receive keys, access codes, or entry devices
Create disruption, unsafe conditions, or conflict

OVERNIGHT GUESTS

Overnight guests are not automatically permitted. Any overnight guest must be approved according to house rules and property expectations.

NO TENANCY OR OCCUPANCY RIGHTS

Guests do not obtain tenancy, residency, program participation, or occupancy rights by visiting the property.

PROPERTY OWNER / HOUSING PROVIDER RIGHTS

The housing provider may restrict or prohibit guests who violate house rules, create disruption, damage property, threaten safety, or interfere with the rights of other participants.

PARTICIPANT ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By signing below, the participant acknowledges that they understand the guest rules and accept responsibility for invited guests.
