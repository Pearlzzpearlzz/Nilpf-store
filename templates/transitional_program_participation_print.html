PURPOSE

This Program Participation Agreement explains the expectations, responsibilities, and program conditions for a participant entering a Transitional Housing Program.

This agreement is used when the participant is being housed as part of a structured transitional housing program, including reentry, VA-aligned, referral-based, or program-supported housing.

This agreement is program-facing. It does not replace the Master Lease, Master License Agreement, house rules, funding documents, referral documents, or required program coordination forms.

PROGRAM STRUCTURE

The Transitional Housing Program may operate under a dual-entity structure.

The property may be owned, leased, or controlled by a separate property entity, such as an LLC.

The program may be operated or managed by a nonprofit, program operator, or coordinating organization.

The Program Operator is responsible for program participation requirements, participant coordination, referral communication, documentation, program expectations, and compliance-related activities.

The property entity is responsible only for the property-related role assigned to it by written agreement.

PARTICIPANT STATUS

The participant understands that transitional housing is program-based housing and not permanent housing.

REFERRAL AND PROGRAM COORDINATION

The participant may be referred by or connected to outside agencies including reentry programs, VA programs, Continuum of Care partners, and social service agencies.

The participant understands that program placement and continuation may depend on referral requirements, documentation, or funding approval.

PARTICIPANT RIGHTS

  • Right to dignity and respect
  • Right to privacy
  • Right to written expectations
  • Right to file grievances without retaliation

PARTICIPANT RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Follow program and house rules
  • Respect others and property
  • Maintain safe environment
  • Participate in required program activities

PROGRAM EXPECTATIONS

The participant may be required to work toward stability goals including employment, housing transition, recovery, or compliance with referral programs.

SAFETY EXPECTATIONS

Violence, illegal activity, or behavior that threatens safety may result in removal from the program.

CONFIDENTIALITY

Participant information will be handled confidentially unless disclosure is required by law or authorized.

GRIEVANCE PROCESS

The participant may submit complaints in writing without fear of retaliation.

PROGRAM REVIEW AND DISCHARGE

Failure to comply with program expectations may result in warnings, corrective action, or discharge.

PROPERTY

The participant is responsible for personal belongings.

NO GUARANTEE

The program does not guarantee permanent housing or funding approval.
