06 – Emergency Evacuation Plan

PURPOSE

This document provides general information regarding emergency evacuation procedures for shared housing program.

This plan is informational only and does not create an obligation to provide emergency services, supervision, or assistance.

NO EMERGENCY RESPONSE SERVICES

shared housing program does not provide:

  • Emergency evacuation assistance
  • Emergency supervision or monitoring
  • Staff-led evacuation procedures
  • Emergency drills or training

Participants are responsible for responding to emergencies and contacting public emergency services as needed.

GENERAL EVACUATION INFORMATION

In the event of an emergency such as fire, natural disaster, or hazardous condition, participants should:

  • Exit the building promptly if it is safe to do so
  • Use available exits and avoid unsafe areas
  • Follow instructions issued by emergency responders
  • Move to a safe location away from the building
  • Participants should call 911 or appropriate emergency services.

Participants should not re-enter the building until authorities indicate it is safe.

EXIT AWARENESS

Participants are encouraged to independently familiarize themselves with:

  • Primary and secondary exits
  • Common pathways of egress
  • Posted emergency signage

The housing provider does not verify or monitor participant awareness of exit locations.

EMERGENCY CONTACT

Participants are responsible for contacting emergency services such as 911 when needed.

Emergency responders have authority during emergency situations.

SELF-DIRECTED RESPONSE

Each participant acknowledges that:

  • Emergency response is self-directed
  • No staff or housing representative is assigned to assist with evacuation
  • Personal safety decisions are the responsibility of the participant

The housing provider does not assume responsibility for individual evacuation outcomes.

NO GUARANTEES

No guarantees are made regarding emergency conditions, evacuation timing, or outcomes.

This plan is provided for general awareness only.

ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By signing below, the participant acknowledges that:

  • This Emergency Evacuation Plan is informational only
  • shared housing program does not provide emergency services or evacuation assistance
  • The participant is responsible for their own emergency response actions
