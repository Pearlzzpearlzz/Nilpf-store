05 – Fire Safety and Self-Preservation Acknowledgement

PURPOSE

This document acknowledges resident awareness of basic fire safety considerations and confirms individual responsibility for personal safety and self-preservation in the event of an emergency.

This acknowledgement applies universally and is not specific to any state or jurisdiction.

NO FIRE SAFETY SERVICES OR SUPERVISION

Independent Living housing does not provide:

  • Fire safety training
  • Emergency response services
  • Supervision or monitoring
  • Assistance with evacuation or rescue

The housing provider does not act as a safety officer, emergency responder, or supervisor.

PARTICIPANT AWARENESS AND RESPONSIBILITY

By participating in Independent Living housing, the participant understands and acknowledges that they are responsible for:

  • Becoming familiar with exit routes and building layout
  • Responding appropriately to alarms or emergency conditions
  • Seeking assistance from emergency services when needed
  • Taking reasonable steps to protect their own safety

Each participant is responsible for their own ability to respond to emergencies.

SELF-PRESERVATION EXPECTATION

Participants acknowledge that Independent Living housing is intended for individuals who are capable of self-preservation.

The housing provider does not assess, monitor, or verify a participant’s ability to respond to emergencies.

EMERGENCY SERVICES

In the event of a fire or emergency, residents are responsible for contacting emergency services (such as 911) as needed.

The housing provider is not responsible for emergency response or evacuation assistance.

NO GUARANTEES

No guarantees are made regarding safety outcomes, emergency response, or individual circumstances.

Participants accept personal responsibility for their own safety decisions.

ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By signing below, the participant acknowledges understanding of and agreement to the following:

  • Independent Living housing does not provide fire safety services or supervision
  • The participant is responsible for personal safety and self-preservation
  • Emergency response is the responsibility of public emergency services
