2. PURPOSE

This Agreement establishes a structured transitional housing arrangement designed to support individuals reentering the community and progressing toward stable, independent living.

The Program provides shared housing within a structured environment focused on accountability, stability, and successful reintegration.

3. NATURE OF OCCUPANCY

Participant is granted a revocable, non-exclusive license to occupy an assigned sleeping space within shared housing.

This Agreement does not create a landlord-tenant relationship, exclusive possession, or permanent housing rights unless required by applicable law.

4. PROGRAM PARTICIPATION

Participant agrees to follow program expectations, communicate with the Program Operator, comply with documentation requirements, and maintain respectful behavior within the shared housing environment.

5. HOUSE RULES AND COMMUNITY STANDARDS

Participant agrees to follow all house rules, safety requirements, quiet hours, cleanliness expectations, guest rules, and shared space responsibilities.

7. NO GUARANTEE OF SERVICES

This Agreement does not create medical, nursing, personal care, supervision, or assisted living obligations.

Any supportive services must be separately documented and provided by an authorized agency or provider.

8. TERMINATION

The Program Operator may terminate this license for violation of program expectations, safety concerns, non-payment where applicable, failure to comply with documentation, or other written program reasons.

9. PARTICIPANT RIGHTS

Participant has the right to fair treatment, privacy within shared housing limits, access to grievance procedures when applicable, and written notice as required by program policy or law.

10. ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Participant acknowledges that this is a license agreement for transitional shared housing and not a traditional lease or tenancy unless applicable law states otherwise.
