Purpose (read first):
This screening confirms an individual’s capacity for self-directed living without services or supervision.
It does not assess worthiness, behavior control, social preferences, or relationship choices.

Section 1 — Core Eligibility (Non-Negotiable)

The applicant must answer YES to all items below.

If any answer is NO → IL is not appropriate at this time.
That is a placement decision, not a judgment.

Section 2 — Functional Self-Management Screen

The applicant must demonstrate ability in the following areas:

A. Daily Living

B. Health & Safety

C. Decision-Making

Section 3 — Housing & Community Compatibility

This is NOT behavior control. It is standard housing suitability.

⚠️ Note:
Emotional expression, relationships, visitors, disagreement, or personal struggles are not disqualifiers.

Section 4 — Visitor & Relationship Affirmation (Critical)

Read carefully. Initial each line.

This section exists to protect independence, not restrict it.

Section 5 — Program Boundaries Acknowledgment

I understand and accept that the IL:

I acknowledge that independence includes freedom and responsibility.

Section 6 — Self-Determination Statement (Signed)

“I am choosing Independent Living voluntarily. I understand that autonomy includes risk, responsibility, and personal decision-making. I do not expect supervision, care, or control from this program.”

Section 7 — Administrative Determination (Staff)

Decision is based solely on functional independence and informed choice.
