Purpose (read first):
This screening confirms an individual’s capacity for self-directed living without services or supervision.
It does not assess worthiness, behavior control, social preferences, or relationship choices.

Why This Tool Is Defensible (Important)

This tool:

  • Confirms capacity, not compliance
  • Protects the program without stripping autonomy
  • Avoids institutional logic
  • Aligns with the true definition of Independent Living
  • Prevents “problem-free by control” practices

This tool explicitly rejects:

  • visitor bans
  • behavioral engineering
  • pre-institutional screening
  • independence theft disguised as safety
