03 – Voluntary Participation Acknowledgement

Shared Housing Program

PURPOSE

This document confirms that participation in shared housing program is voluntary and based on personal choice.

This acknowledgement applies universally and is not specific to any state or jurisdiction.

VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION

Participation in shared housing program is entirely voluntary.

Individuals choose to participate based on their own judgment, needs, and preferences. No person is required, compelled, or mandated to reside in shared housing program.

Participation is not a condition of receiving services, benefits, treatment, or referrals from any third party.

NO PLACEMENT OR REQUIREMENT

shared housing program is not:

  • A placement program
  • A mandated residence
  • A requirement of probation, parole, treatment, or services
  • A condition of eligibility for benefits or assistance

No government agency, service provider, referral source, or third party has authority to require participation.

RIGHT TO DECLINE OR DISCONTINUE

Participants may decline participation or choose to discontinue residence in shared housing program at any time, subject only to applicable housing terms and shared living standards.

There are no penalties, sanctions, or consequences imposed by the housing provider for choosing not to participate.

INDEPENDENT DECISION-MAKING

By participating, the individual affirms that:

  • The decision to reside in shared housing program was made freely
  • No promises, guarantees, or outcomes were offered or implied
  • No pressure, coercion, or inducement was used

Participants understand that shared housing program does not ensure specific results or outcomes.

  • Residence is not required by any authority
  • They may decline or discontinue participation at their discretion
