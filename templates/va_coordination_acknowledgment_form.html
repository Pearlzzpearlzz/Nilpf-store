PURPOSE

This document confirms the participant’s understanding of how housing and external program coordination may occur within a transitional housing environment.

This housing setting may coordinate, when applicable, with:

  • U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs
  • Grant and Per Diem Program
  • Reentry or community-based programs

This acknowledgment is informational only. It does not establish a service, supervisory, custodial, clinical, or program relationship with the housing provider.

STRUCTURE OF HOUSING AND PROGRAMS

The participant understands that this environment may involve a dual-entity structure:

  • The Housing Provider (LLC) is responsible for the physical residence and property operations.
  • A Program Operator (501(c)(3)), if applicable, may provide supportive services separately.

These entities operate independently, even when limited coordination occurs.

PROGRAM PARTICIPATION

The participant understands:

  • Participation in any external program is voluntary and independent.
  • Any case management, supportive services, program requirements, or compliance expectations are provided and enforced solely by the program operator or external agency.
  • The housing provider does not require, manage, supervise, monitor, or enforce participation in any external program.

ACCESS TO SERVICES WITHIN HOUSING

External service providers, including those affiliated with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs or programs such as the Grant and Per Diem Program, may interact with the participant within or in connection with the housing environment.

Such interactions may include case management visits, program check-ins, housing stability discussions, or other supportive services provided by external entities.

The participant understands that:

  • All services are provided independently by external providers.
  • The housing provider does not deliver, supervise, coordinate, or control these services.
  • The housing provider does not enforce program participation or program compliance.
  • Access to services within the housing does not create a service relationship with the housing provider.

ROLE OF THE HOUSING PROVIDER (LLC)

The participant acknowledges that the housing provider:

  • Provides housing only.
  • Maintains property standards and general safety expectations.
  • Handles property-related matters connected to the residence.

The housing provider does not provide:

  • Case management
  • Clinical, medical, treatment, or therapeutic services
  • Supervision, monitoring, or daily living oversight
  • Program oversight or program compliance tracking
  • Financial management or representative payee services

The housing provider does not function as a service provider.

INFORMATION SHARING & COORDINATION

The participant may authorize limited coordination between the Program Operator, VA or affiliated programs, community-based programs, and the Housing Provider.

If authorized, coordination is limited to:

  • Housing verification
  • Residency confirmation
  • Move-in or move-out confirmation
  • Basic communication necessary for housing stability

The housing provider does not provide ongoing behavioral reports, clinical updates, program compliance reports, or service-related records unless separately required by law or specifically authorized in writing.

FINANCIAL UNDERSTANDING

The participant understands:

  • Housing costs may be supported through external program funding, including VA-related or reentry-aligned funding where applicable.
  • Participant contributions may apply depending on program structure, funding source, and written agreement.
  • The housing provider does not manage, control, or oversee participant finances.
  • Any payment arrangement should be documented separately from this acknowledgment.

INDEPENDENCE & RESPONSIBILITY

The participant remains fully responsible for:

  • Personal decisions
  • Healthcare choices
  • Finances
  • Daily living activities
  • Communication with program providers or external agencies

This housing environment does not create a custodial, clinical, institutional, treatment, or supervised living relationship.
