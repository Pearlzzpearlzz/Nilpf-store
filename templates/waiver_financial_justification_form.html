1. PURPOSE OF REQUEST

The Program respectfully requests consideration for an enhanced per diem, allotment, reimbursement, or funding rate based on the actual operating costs required to maintain a structured transitional housing environment.

The Program provides shared residential housing designed to support stability, accountability, documentation, housing readiness, and successful reintegration into the community.

Due to the structure of the housing model, shared occupancy needs, compliance documentation, administrative coordination, and local housing costs, standard rates may be insufficient to sustain program operations at the required level of quality and consistency.

5. LIMITATIONS OF STANDARD RATE

Standard per diem or reimbursement rates may be expected to cover housing, administrative coordination, documentation, participant intake, reporting requirements, program structure, utilities, maintenance, and operational overhead.

Due to local housing costs, inflation, shared housing wear and tear, and compliance-related requirements, base rates may not fully support program sustainability while maintaining safe, stable, and organized housing operations.

6. JUSTIFICATION FOR ENHANCED RATE

A. Shared Housing Complexity

Shared housing requires increased coordination, maintenance, communication, and replacement costs due to multiple occupants.

B. Documentation and Compliance

The Program maintains structured documentation, participant tracking, reporting support, intake records, housing records, and operational files.

C. Program Stability

The Program is designed to provide a structured environment focused on accountability, housing stability, and progression toward independence.

D. Cost of Safe Housing

Utilities, maintenance, cleaning, furnishings, and household replacement costs are elevated in shared housing environments.

E. Reentry / VA / Publicly Funded Alignment

Where applicable, this justification may support review by programs, referral partners, funders, or administrative entities requiring cost documentation.
