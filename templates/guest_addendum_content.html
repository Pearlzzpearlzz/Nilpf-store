09 – Guest Addendum

Shared Housing Program

PURPOSE

This Guest Addendum outlines expectations and responsibilities related to guests within shared housing.

This document applies to shared housing only and does not create supervision, services, or program oversight.

GUEST DEFINITION

A guest is any individual invited onto the premises by a participant who is not an approved participant/member of the housing.

PARTICIPANT RESPONSIBILITY FOR GUESTS

Participants are solely responsible for their guests, including:

  • Guest behavior and conduct
  • Compliance with house rules and community standards
  • Any damage caused by the guest
  • Ensuring guests do not interfere with the rights of others

The housing provider does not supervise or manage guests.

GUEST CONDUCT EXPECTATIONS

Guests are expected to:

  • Respect the shared living environment
  • Comply with house rules and applicable laws
  • Avoid disruptive, unsafe, or illegal behavior

Guests who do not comply may be required to leave the premises.

LIMITATIONS AND RESTRICTIONS

Shared housing may establish reasonable limitations related to:

  • Number of guests
  • Duration of visits
  • Overnight stays

Such limitations are housing-related and do not constitute supervision or services.

NO TENANCY OR RIGHTS CREATED

Guest access does not create:

  • Residency rights
  • Tenancy or occupancy status
  • Any legal or contractual relationship with the housing provider

Guests have no independent right to remain on the premises.

NO SUPERVISION OR MONITORING

The housing provider does not:

  • Monitor guest activity
  • Enforce guest behavior beyond housing standards
  • Provide security or supervision

Responsibility remains with the participant who invited the guest.

ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By signing below, the participant acknowledges understanding of and agreement to this Guest Addendum.
