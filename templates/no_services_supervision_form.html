02 – No Services / No Supervision Acknowledgement

Independent Living Housing | Universal Use

PURPOSE OF THIS ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

This document confirms the participant’s understanding that the Independent Living housing model is housing-only and does not include services, supervision, or care of any kind.

This acknowledgement applies universally and is not specific to any state or jurisdiction.

NO SERVICES PROVIDED

The Independent Living Program Owner and/or Housing Provider does not provide, arrange, coordinate, or oversee any of the following:

  • Medical services
  • Behavioral health services
  • Mental health services
  • Substance use treatment or recovery services
  • Personal care or assistance
  • Case management or support services
  • Transportation, medication management, or daily living assistance

Any services an individual may choose to obtain are arranged independently and are not affiliated with the housing provider.

NO SUPERVISION OR MONITORING

The housing provider does not:

  • Supervise residents or participants
  • Monitor behavior, activities, or compliance
  • Enforce treatment plans or recovery programs
  • Assign duties or manage daily schedules
  • Intervene in personal or interpersonal matters

Participants are responsible for managing their own conduct and decisions as independent adults.

NOT A CARE OR TREATMENT SETTING

This Independent Living housing arrangement is not:

  • A treatment facility
  • A recovery or rehabilitation program
  • A group home or supervised living environment
  • A licensed service provider
  • An institutional or clinical setting

Participation does not constitute enrollment in any care, treatment, or service program.

PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITY

Each participant acknowledges that they:

  • Are responsible for their own personal, medical, and daily living needs
  • Must arrange any desired services independently
  • Are not relying on the housing provider for care, supervision, or assistance

The housing provider assumes no responsibility for individual outcomes or personal circumstances.

ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By signing associated documentation, the participant acknowledges and agrees that:

  • No services or supervision are provided
  • The housing arrangement is independent and voluntary
  • The housing provider does not assume a caregiving or supervisory role

End of Acknowledgement
