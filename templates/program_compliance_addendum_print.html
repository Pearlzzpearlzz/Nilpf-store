1. APPLICABILITY

This Addendum applies when a participant is placed within the Program through:

U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs programs, including Grant and Per Diem or VA-aligned referrals

Continuum of Care (CoC) Program placements

Correctional reentry, probation, parole, DOC, or justice-involved referral pathways

Any publicly funded, referral-based, or program-supported housing pathway requiring enhanced compliance standards

This Addendum supplements the Master License Agreement and governs only in such cases.

This Addendum is intended to document program-level compliance alignment only. It does not create, expand, or modify housing rights, ownership rights, tenancy rights, or property-control rights.

2. NATURE OF OCCUPANCY AND PROGRAM ALIGNMENT

The Program maintains a structured transitional housing model designed to coordinate with referral, funding, documentation, and program-alignment expectations.

The Program is structured to remain compatible with multiple referral systems simultaneously while maintaining a uniform housing framework.

Participation in this Program may be connected to an outside referral source, funding source, case manager, VA representative, CoC partner, DOC/reentry contact, probation/parole contact, or other approved coordinating agency.

The Program Operator may maintain records, signed forms, participation notes, incident documentation, referral documentation, discharge notes, and coordination records when necessary for program alignment.

3. PARTICIPANT RIGHTS AND PROTECTIONS

The participant has the right to:

Fair and non-discriminatory treatment

Written notice of program expectations

Reasonable notice prior to standard program termination when applicable

Access to a grievance or complaint process

Privacy and dignity consistent with program rules and applicable law

Freedom from retaliation for submitting a grievance or complaint

4. NOTICE AND TERMINATION PROCEDURES

Standard program termination may require reasonable notice depending on applicable law, funding requirements, referral requirements, or written program policy.

Immediate program action may occur when there is a serious safety risk, violence, illegal activity, weapons, severe property damage, threats, or conduct that creates urgent risk to others.

Program removal or discharge does not by itself determine housing status. Housing status is governed separately by the applicable Master License Agreement or other housing agreement.

5. GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE

Participants may submit written grievances regarding:

Program conditions

Rule enforcement

Program decisions

Discharge concerns

Staff conduct

The Program Operator will review grievances within a reasonable time and may document the concern, response, and any corrective action taken.

If the participant was referred by an outside agency, the participant may also contact the referral source or coordinating agency when appropriate.

6. PROGRAM PARTICIPATION REQUIREMENTS

The participant agrees to:

Engage in written program expectations

Participate in coordination when applicable

Comply with house standards and safety expectations

Cooperate with reasonable documentation requests

Attend scheduled meetings, check-ins, or reviews when required

Maintain respectful conduct toward others and the property

The participant understands that referral-based or publicly funded placement may require:

Documentation requests

Scheduled check-ins

Eligibility reviews

Transition planning

Case coordination

Program-status updates

The participant remains responsible for complying with requirements imposed by:

External agencies

Courts

Probation/parole

VA programs

Funding sources

Referral partners

The Program Operator may document participation, communicate with authorized sources, and maintain records necessary to preserve program alignment.

7. VA / DOC / REENTRY / COC AND PROGRAM COORDINATION

The Program Operator may coordinate with:

Case managers

VA representatives

CoC partners

Probation/parole contacts

DOC or reentry contacts

Funding administrators

Service providers

This coordination occurs only when authorized by release, referral agreement, funding requirement, or applicable law.

The Program Operator does not act as:

Law enforcement

Probation or parole officer

Clinical or treatment provider

VA decision-maker

Benefits approval authority

The Program Operator may provide housing-related and program-participation documentation when properly authorized.

8. FINANCIAL STRUCTURE CLARIFICATION

Program funding, referral support, grants, per diem payments, participant contributions, or other approved funding sources may support housing-related program costs when applicable.

Nothing in this Addendum guarantees:

Funding approval

VA approval

Grant approval

Public assistance

Rental assistance

Benefits approval

Permanent housing

Continued program placement

The Program Operator may assist with coordination and documentation, but final decisions are made by outside entities.

9. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW

This Addendum shall be interpreted in accordance with applicable federal, state, and local law.

If any outside requirement conflicts with applicable law or protected rights, applicable law controls.

10. RELATIONSHIP TO MASTER LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Addendum supplements the Master License Agreement for participants placed through referral-based or publicly funded pathways.

Program participation and housing occupancy are related but distinct matters.

This Addendum governs program compliance alignment only.

Housing status is governed separately by the applicable housing agreement.

Nothing in this Addendum creates:

A landlord-tenant relationship

Independent housing rights

A clinical or custodial relationship

A guarantee of continued placement

11. ACKNOWLEDGMENT

They understand when this Addendum applies

They understand external obligations remain their responsibility

They understand the Program Operator may coordinate with authorized entities

They understand this Addendum does not replace housing agreements
