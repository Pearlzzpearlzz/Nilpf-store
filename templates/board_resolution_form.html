Standing Board Resolution

Authorization for Housing Program Agreements

PURPOSE

This Resolution establishes standing authority for the Organization to enter into housing-related agreements necessary to support transitional housing, reentry housing, VA-aligned housing coordination, and other program-based residential arrangements.

STANDING AUTHORIZATION

The Board authorizes the Organization to enter into agreements with property-owning entities, including affiliated entities where disclosed, for the purpose of securing and operating program-based housing.

DISCLOSURE OF RELATIONSHIPS

The Board acknowledges that agreements may involve related entities or individuals serving in more than one role. Any such relationship should be disclosed and documented to support transparency.

BOARD ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

☐ The Board authorizes housing-related agreements for program purposes

☐ The Board acknowledges possible dual-entity relationships when disclosed

☐ The Board authorizes officers or designated representatives to execute related documents
