Independent Living – Shared Occupancy (Non-Tenancy / Non-Care)

This Master Program License Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between Operator and the undersigned Participant (“Participant”) for participation in an independent shared housing arrangement. This Agreement establishes a revocable license to occupy and does not constitute a lease, rental agreement, or tenancy.

1. Purpose of Agreement

The purpose of this Agreement is to grant Participant permission to occupy an assigned sleeping space within a shared living environment solely as an incident of participation in an independent living program.

This Agreement is intended to:

  • Facilitate temporary, conditional occupancy
  • Preserve participant autonomy
  • Avoid creation of any landlord–tenant relationship
  • Avoid provision of care, supervision, or assistance
  • Operate outside residential tenancy and housing licensure frameworks

2. Nature of the Legal Relationship

License to Occupy — Not a Lease

Participant is granted a revocable, non-exclusive license to occupy an assigned sleeping space within a shared residence.

This Agreement:

  • Does not convey exclusive possession
  • Does not create a property interest or estate
  • Does not grant tenancy rights
  • Does not create a landlord–tenant relationship
  • Does not constitute a rental or lease agreement

Permission to occupy exists only while this license remains in effect.

3. Independent Living Declaration (No Care / No Supervision)

Participant acknowledges and agrees that:

  • The Program provides independent living only
  • Operator does not provide:
    • Supervision
    • Monitoring
    • Care
    • Treatment
    • Assistance
    • Case management
    • Medication management
    • Behavioral oversight
  • Participant is fully independent and self-directed
  • Participant retains full responsibility for:
    • Personal care
    • Medical and behavioral health needs
    • Medications
    • Appointments
    • Transportation
    • Daily living decisions

Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as:

  • Assisted living
  • Group care
  • Residential care
  • Supportive housing services
  • Medical or custodial services

4. Shared Occupancy & Space Assignment

Participant is assigned a sleeping space, not a private dwelling unit.

Participant understands:

  • Rooms are shared
  • Beds may be reassigned at any time
  • Operator retains full control over space allocation
  • No room, bed, or area is permanently assigned
  • Occupancy is communal by design

This structure is fundamental to maintaining non-tenancy status.

5. Program Rules (Administrative Only)

Participant agrees to comply with administrative house standards necessary for shared living, including rules related to:

  • Safety
  • Cleanliness
  • Noise
  • Respect for shared space
  • Prohibited conduct

These rules:

  • Do not constitute supervision
  • Do not regulate personal routines
  • Do not involve monitoring or oversight of daily activities
  • Exist solely to manage shared property use

6. Payment — Program Participation Contribution

Not Rent

Any payment made in connection with this Agreement is a program participation contribution, not rent.

Payment:

  • Does not purchase housing rights
  • Does not guarantee continued occupancy
  • Does not create tenancy
  • Does not establish exclusive possession

Source of Payment

Payments may be made by:

  • Participant (including from SSI, SSDI, or other benefits)
  • A representative payee
  • A third-party organization
  • A combination of the above

The source, method, or timing of payment does not alter the nature of this Agreement as a license.

7. No Security Deposit

No funds are accepted or held as a security deposit.

Any administrative or program fees:

  • Are not housing deposits
  • Are not prepaid rent
  • Do not confer occupancy rights

8. Revocation of License

Because this Agreement is a license and not a lease:

Permission to occupy may be revoked upon failure to comply with the terms of this Agreement or applicable Program standards.

  • Revocation is not an eviction
  • Residential landlord–tenant eviction procedures do not apply
  • Participant has no right to remain based on payment, duration, or expectation

Upon revocation, Participant must vacate the premises promptly.

9. No Guarantee of Placement or Duration

Participant acknowledges:

  • Occupancy is temporary and conditional
  • No minimum or maximum duration is promised
  • Continued permission depends on ongoing compliance

10. Risk Acknowledgment

Participant acknowledges that shared independent living involves inherent risks and agrees to assume responsibility for personal safety, belongings, and conduct.

Operator is not responsible for:

  • Personal property loss
  • Participant disputes
  • Independent actions of others

11. Fair Housing & Disability Rights

Nothing in this Agreement waives rights under:

  • The Fair Housing Act
  • The Americans with Disabilities Act
  • Applicable state or local civil rights laws

This Program operates on a non-discriminatory basis.

12. No Waiver / No Reliance

Failure by Operator to enforce any provision does not waive the right to enforce later.

Participant acknowledges no reliance on:

  • Oral promises
  • Representations outside this Agreement
  • Expectations of tenancy or permanence

13. Severability & Intent

If any provision is recharacterized or deemed unenforceable:

  • Remaining provisions remain in effect
  • This Agreement shall be interpreted to preserve a license-based, non-tenancy relationship
  • No provision shall be expanded to create tenancy or care obligations

14. State Addendum Incorporated

This Agreement incorporates by reference any State-Specific License Addendum attached hereto, which addresses governing law and jurisdictional considerations without altering the license-based nature of this Agreement.

15. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with any attached addenda, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding Program participation and occupancy permission.
