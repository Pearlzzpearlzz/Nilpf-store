01 – Independent Living Program Disclosure

Version 2.1 — Revised February 2026

Housing-Only Model | Universal Use

PURPOSE OF THIS DISCLOSURE

This document provides a clear explanation of the nature and scope of the Independent Living housing model. It is intended to ensure informed participation and to establish boundaries between housing access and services.

This disclosure applies universally and is not specific to any state or jurisdiction.

NATURE OF THE INDEPENDENT LIVING MODEL

The Independent Living model is a housing-only arrangement that provides shared living access to adults who manage their own daily lives.

Participation in this model does not include services, care, supervision, treatment, or programmatic oversight.

NO SERVICES / NO SUPERVISION

The Independent Living Program Owner and/or Housing Provider:

  • Does not provide medical, behavioral, clinical, or therapeutic services
  • Does not provide personal care, assistance, or supervision
  • Does not manage daily schedules, medications, finances, or activities
  • Does not assign duties, enforce treatment plans, or monitor behavior
  • Does not act as a caregiver, case manager, sponsor, or provider

All participants are responsible for their own needs, decisions, and arrangements.

NOT A CARE, TREATMENT, OR PLACEMENT PROGRAM

  • A treatment program
  • A recovery or rehabilitation program
  • A group home, facility, or institution
  • A placement or referral service
  • A licensed service provider

Participation does not constitute enrollment in any program of care or services.

VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION

Participation in Independent Living housing is voluntary.

Individuals choose to participate based on their own assessment of suitability and compatibility with a shared living environment. No guarantees of outcomes, success, or duration are made or implied.

PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITY

  • Manage their own personal, medical, financial, and daily living needs
  • Arrange their own services, supports, or assistance if desired
  • Comply with applicable laws and shared housing standards

The housing provider does not assume responsibility for personal outcomes or individual circumstances.

NO LEGAL OR PROFESSIONAL ADVICE

This disclosure is informational only and does not constitute legal, medical, or professional advice.

ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By reviewing and signing associated documentation, the participant acknowledges understanding of:

  • The housing-only nature of the Independent Living model
  • The absence of services, care, or supervision
  • Their personal responsibility for independent living decisions

End of Disclosure
