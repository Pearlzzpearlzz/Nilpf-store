PROGRAM COMPLIANCE ADDENDUM

(VA / DOC / Reentry / CoC / Publicly Funded Transitional Housing Alignment Addendum)
NILPF Framework – 2026

1. APPLICABILITY

This Addendum applies when a participant is placed within the Program through:

  • U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs programs, including Grant and Per Diem or VA-aligned referrals
  • Continuum of Care (CoC) Program placements
  • Correctional reentry, probation, parole, DOC, or justice-involved referral pathways
  • Any publicly funded, referral-based, or program-supported housing pathway requiring enhanced compliance standards

This Addendum supplements the Master License Agreement and governs only in such cases.

This Addendum is intended to document program-level compliance alignment only. It does not create, expand, or modify housing rights, ownership rights, tenancy rights, or property-control rights.

2. NATURE OF OCCUPANCY AND PROGRAM ALIGNMENT

The Program maintains a structured transitional housing model designed to coordinate with referral, funding, documentation, and program-alignment expectations.

The Program is structured to remain compatible with multiple referral systems simultaneously while maintaining a uniform housing framework.

Participation in this Program may be connected to an outside referral source, funding source, case manager, VA representative, CoC partner, DOC/reentry contact, probation/parole contact, or other approved coordinating agency.

The Program Operator may maintain records, signed forms, participation notes, incident documentation, referral documentation, discharge notes, and coordination records when necessary for program alignment.

3. PARTICIPANT RIGHTS AND PROTECTIONS

  • Fair and non-discriminatory treatment
  • Written notice of program expectations
  • Reasonable notice prior to standard program termination when applicable
  • Access to a grievance or complaint process
  • Privacy and dignity consistent with program rules and applicable law
  • Freedom from retaliation for submitting a grievance or complaint

4. NOTICE AND TERMINATION PROCEDURES

Standard program termination may require reasonable notice depending on applicable law, funding requirements, referral requirements, or written program policy.

Immediate program action may occur when there is a serious safety risk, violence, illegal activity, weapons, severe property damage, threats, or conduct that creates urgent risk to others.

Program removal or discharge does not by itself determine housing status. Housing status is governed separately by the applicable Master License Agreement or other housing agreement.

5. GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE

Participants may submit written grievances regarding program conditions, rule enforcement, program decisions, discharge concerns, or staff conduct.

The Program Operator will review grievances within a reasonable time and may document the concern, response, and any corrective action taken.

If the participant was referred by an outside agency, the participant may also contact the referral source or coordinating agency when appropriate.

6. PROGRAM PARTICIPATION REQUIREMENTS

  • Engage in written program expectations
  • Participate in coordination when applicable
  • Comply with house standards and safety expectations
  • Cooperate with reasonable documentation requests
  • Attend scheduled meetings, check-ins, or reviews when required by the program pathway
  • Maintain respectful conduct toward other participants, staff, neighbors, and property

The participant understands that referral-based or publicly funded placement may require cooperation with reasonable documentation requests, scheduled check-ins, eligibility reviews, transition planning, case coordination, and program-status updates.

The participant remains responsible for complying with requirements imposed by external agencies, courts, probation/parole, VA programs, funding sources, or referral partners.

The Program Operator may document participation, communicate with authorized referral or funding sources, and maintain records necessary to preserve program alignment.

7. VA / DOC / REENTRY / COC AND PROGRAM COORDINATION

The Program Operator may coordinate with approved referral sources, case managers, VA representatives, CoC partners, probation/parole contacts, DOC or reentry contacts, funding administrators, or service providers when authorized by release, referral agreement, funding requirement, or applicable law.

The Program Operator does not act as a law enforcement agency, probation officer, parole officer, clinical provider, treatment provider, VA decision-maker, or benefits approval authority.

The Program Operator may provide housing-related and program-participation documentation when properly authorized or required by the participant pathway.

8. FINANCIAL STRUCTURE CLARIFICATION

Program funding, referral support, grants, per diem payments, participant contributions, or other approved funding sources may support housing-related program costs when applicable.

Nothing in this Addendum guarantees funding approval, VA approval, grant approval, public assistance approval, rental assistance, benefits approval, permanent housing placement, or continued program payment.

The Program Operator may assist with coordination and documentation, but final decisions may be made by outside agencies, funders, government programs, landlords, or referral systems.

9. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW

This Addendum shall be interpreted consistent with applicable federal, state, and local law.

If any outside program requirement conflicts with applicable law or the participant’s protected rights, applicable law controls.

10. RELATIONSHIP TO MASTER LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Addendum supplements the Master License Agreement for participants placed through referral-based, publicly funded, VA-aligned, DOC/reentry, CoC, or program-supported pathways.

Program participation and housing occupancy are related but distinct matters. This Addendum governs program compliance alignment only. Housing status is governed separately by the applicable Master License Agreement or other housing agreement.

Nothing in this Addendum creates a landlord-tenant relationship, independent housing right, clinical service relationship, custodial relationship, or guarantee of continued placement.

11. ACKNOWLEDGMENT

By signing below, the participant acknowledges that:

  • They have received or been given access to this Addendum
  • They understand this Addendum applies only when their placement involves enhanced program, referral, funding, VA, DOC/reentry, CoC, or public-program alignment
  • They understand their external agency obligations remain their responsibility
  • They understand the Program Operator may coordinate with approved entities when properly authorized
  • They understand this Addendum does not replace the Master License Agreement or other applicable housing documents
